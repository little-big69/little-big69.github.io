What the Application does

Beemy shows energy data from a Beem solar kit: solar production, battery state, home consumption, and grid import and export. With an optional Beemy account, it also shows a solar forecast for your installation.


What information is processed
  • Beem account: your Beem email address and password. They are sent to Beem's API (api-x.beem.energy) to sign you in and to load your energy data. The password is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device so the app can sign in again. A Beem access token is kept on your device and is used by the widgets and the Apple Watch app.
  • Beemy account (optional): an email address and password used to sign in with Google Firebase Authentication. The app does not keep the password itself. The Beemy account is used to sync your Solcast settings and to get solar forecasts.
  • Solcast settings: your Solcast API key and rooftop site IDs, which you enter in the app.
  • Installation location: the address and coordinates of your installation, which you choose on a map or by searching for an address. The app does not use your device's GPS location.

Data stored on your device

Your Beem sign-in details, Solcast settings, installation address and coordinates, app preferences, and cached solar forecast data are stored on your device.


Data sent to the Service Provider's server

When you are signed in to a Beemy account, the app sends your Firebase sign-in token and your Solcast settings to the Service Provider's server (beemy.little-big.eu). The server stores your Firebase user ID, your Solcast API key and rooftop site IDs, and a cache of forecast data. It does not store your email address. The server uses your Solcast API key to request forecasts from Solcast.


Third-party services
  • Beem (api-x.beem.energy and mqtt.beem.energy): your sign-in and your energy data. Beem's own privacy terms apply.
  • Google Firebase Authentication: sign-in to the optional Beemy account. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Solcast (api.solcast.com.au): solar forecasts, requested by the Service Provider's server with your API key. Solcast's own privacy terms apply.
  • Apple Maps: address search and address lookup for the installation location. See Apple's privacy policy.

These services may process data outside your country under their own privacy terms.


Advertising, analytics and tracking

The Application shows no advertising, includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, does not track you across other apps and websites, and has no in-app purchases.


Permissions

The Application does not ask for access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or notifications. It needs an internet connection.


Data retention and deletion
  • On your device: the Beem sign-in is removed when you sign out of Beem in the app. Settings, installation details, and cached forecast data are removed when you delete the app. Items in the iOS Keychain may remain there after the app is deleted.
  • On the Service Provider's server: if you are signed in to a Beemy account and clear your Solcast API key in the app, your Solcast settings and cached forecast data are deleted from the server. Signing out of the Beemy account does not delete them.
  • Deleting your Beemy account: to delete your Beemy account and the data stored for it on the server, email little.big69@icloud.com from the address used for the account. The request will be handled within the time required by applicable law.

Security

Connections to Beem's API, to the Service Provider's server, and to Solcast use HTTPS. Your Beem password is stored in the iOS Keychain. No security system can prevent every possible breach.


Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact little.big69@icloud.com so it can be deleted.
