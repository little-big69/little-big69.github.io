spaceback

Keep the moments. Lose the weight. spaceback compresses the big videos already in your photo library, on your device, and lets you compare before and after before anything is replaced.

Support

Something not working, or a question? Write to little.big69@icloud.com. Mentioning your device model and its iOS or Android version helps.

Common questions

Does my video leave my device? No. Compression happens on your device.

What happens to the original? Nothing until you review the compressed version and choose "Replace original". Your device then asks you to confirm the deletion.

I bought the lifetime unlock on another device. Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases".

A video isn't offered for compression. Tap + in the Library tab to pick it yourself.
